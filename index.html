Frequently Asked Questions

What types of VA claims can you help with?

I can assist with various types of VA claims, including:

  • Disability compensation claims
  • Pension claims
  • Appeals for denied claims
  • Claims for increased disability ratings
  • Dependency and indemnity compensation
  • Healthcare eligibility claims
How long does the VA claims process typically take?

The VA claims process can vary significantly depending on the complexity of your claim, the type of claim, and current VA processing times. Initial claims typically take 3-5 months, but can take longer. With my assistance, we can ensure your claim is properly prepared and documented to avoid unnecessary delays.

What documents do I need to submit a VA claim?

Important documents for VA claims typically include:

  • DD-214 (Certificate of Release or Discharge from Active Duty)
  • Medical records (both military and civilian)
  • Service treatment records
  • Supporting statements from family, friends, or fellow service members
  • VA medical examinations

Don't worry if you don't have all these documents - I can help you identify what you need and how to obtain them.

Is there a fee for your assistance?

I provide assistance to veterans at no cost. My goal is to ensure you receive the benefits you've earned through your service. This is my way of giving back to those who have served our country.

What if my claim has been denied?

If your claim has been denied, don't lose hope. Many claims are initially denied but later approved on appeal. I can help you understand the reason for the denial, gather additional evidence if needed, and guide you through the appeals process. With the right approach, we can work to overturn the denial and secure the benefits you deserve.
